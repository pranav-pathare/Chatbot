# Display & Video 360 (DV360)

## What It Is

Display & Video 360 is the demand-side platform (DSP) of the Google Marketing
Platform, used to plan, buy, and manage programmatic advertising across display,
video, audio, and connected TV. It replaced DoubleClick Bid Manager (DBM) and gives
media buyers a single environment to run real-time bidding campaigns, manage
audiences and creatives, access inventory, and analyze performance.

## Platform Structure

DV360 is organized as **partner > advertiser > campaign > insertion order > line
item**. A partner is the top-level agency or trading desk account; advertisers sit
beneath it; campaigns group related activity; insertion orders (IOs) hold budget and
flight settings that map to the media plan; and line items carry the actual
targeting, bidding, and creatives that bid into auctions. The tool is divided into
modules for Campaigns, Audiences, Inventory, Creatives, and Insights.

## Inventory Sources and Deals

DV360 buys from the open auction (real-time bidding across many exchanges) and from
negotiated **deals**. Deal types include **Programmatic Guaranteed** (fixed price and
guaranteed volume with a publisher), **Preferred Deals** (a fixed price with no volume
commitment), and **Private Auctions** (invite-only auctions at a floor price). Deals
give buyers more control over inventory quality and pricing than the open auction.

## Targeting, Audiences, and Bidding

Line items can target by geography, device, language, content category, and more.
Audiences can be built from first-party data, Floodlight and Google Analytics data,
third-party segments, or Google's own affinity and in-market audiences, and combined
with include/exclude logic. Bidding can be manual (a fixed max CPM) or **automated**,
where DV360 optimizes toward a goal such as target CPA, maximizing conversions, or
hitting a viewability or CPV target within the budget.

## Brand Safety and Frequency

Brand safety controls let buyers exclude sensitive content, apply third-party
verification, and enforce viewability thresholds. Frequency management caps how
often a user is exposed across line items, insertion orders, or the whole campaign,
which protects user experience and spreads reach rather than over-serving a small
group of users.

## Creatives and Reporting

DV360 hosts or references creatives across formats including standard display, video
(in-stream and out-stream), native, and audio. Reporting is available through
offline report builder and the Insights module, with metrics such as impressions,
clicks, CTR, viewable impressions, completed views, conversions, and total media
cost. Because DV360 shares audiences and Floodlight with Campaign Manager 360, buys
measured there can be optimized against the same conversion definitions used across
the media plan.
