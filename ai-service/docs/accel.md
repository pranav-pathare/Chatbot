# IT Asset Allocation SOP (Standard Operating Procedure)

> Document: `ADMPL_SOP_IT_Asset_Allocation_v1.0` | Version: 1.0
> Document Owner: IT Department | Classification: Internal Use Only

## 1. Purpose

Establish a standardized process for requesting, approving, allocating, managing, and
tracking IT assets within the organization. This ensures proper utilization of assets,
accountability, and compliance with internal IT policies and audit requirements.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all employees, contractors, and departments requiring IT assets for official
work purposes. IT assets include but are not limited to:

- Windows Laptops and Apple MacBooks
- Monitors and Docking Stations
- Keyboards and Mouse
- Software Licenses
- VPN and Network Access
- External Storage Devices
- Other IT peripherals

## 3. Roles and Responsibilities

**Requestor (Employee)**
- Submit requests for IT assets through the approved request system.
- Provide necessary justification for the asset.
- Use allocated assets responsibly.

**Reporting Manager**
- Review and approve asset requests based on business needs.

**IT Support Team**
- Validate requests and verify asset availability.
- Prepare and allocate IT assets.
- Maintain records of asset allocation.

**IT Asset Manager**
- Maintain the IT asset inventory.
- Track asset lifecycle and status.
- Conduct periodic asset audits.

## 4. IT Asset Allocation Process

**Step 1 — Asset Request Submission.** The employee submits an IT asset request via email
to IT Support with: Employee Name, Employee ID, Department, Asset Type Required, Business
Justification, and Manager Approval.

**Step 2 — Request Approval.** The reporting manager reviews and approves based on business
requirements. If rejected, the process ends and the employee is notified.

**Step 3 — IT Request Verification.** The IT team verifies availability of the asset in
inventory, compliance with company IT policies, and employee eligibility. If unavailable,
procurement may be initiated.

**Step 4 — Asset Preparation.** The IT team prepares the asset: OS installation, required
applications, antivirus/endpoint protection, domain configuration, security policy
configuration, and asset tagging.

**Step 5 — Asset Allocation.** The IT team issues the asset and records allocation details
(Asset ID, Asset Type, Serial Number, Employee Name, Department, Allocation Date, Asset
Status) in the IT Asset Management System.

**Step 6 — Asset Acknowledgement.** The employee signs
`ADMPL_IT_FRM_IT_Asset_Allocation_v1.0`, confirming receipt of the asset, responsibility
for its care, and compliance with IT usage policies.

## 5. Asset Tracking and Inventory Management

All IT assets must be recorded in the **ADMPL_IT Asset Register** containing: Asset ID,
Asset Name and Category, Serial Number, Asset Owner, Assigned User, Location, Issue Date,
and Status (Available / Allocated / Repair / Retired). Regular inventory reviews should be
conducted by the IT department.

## 6. Asset Maintenance

The IT department is responsible for system updates and patches, hardware maintenance,
troubleshooting technical issues, and replacement of defective assets if necessary.
Employees must raise IT support tickets for issues.

## 7. Asset Reallocation

Assets may be reassigned if the employee changes department, the employee's role changes, or
the asset is no longer required. All reassignments must be updated in the asset inventory
system.

## 8. Asset Return Process

Employees must return allocated IT assets on resignation or termination, role change, asset
upgrade or replacement, or IT department request. Returned assets will be inspected and
updated in the asset register.

## 9. Lost or Damaged Assets

Employees must immediately report any lost, stolen, or damaged IT assets to the IT
department. For lost or stolen assets, employees must file a police complaint and submit a
copy to IT. For damaged assets, employees must provide a written explanation of the
incident. The IT team may take actions such as device lock or remote wipe, security incident
reporting, and replacement or investigation.

## 10. Documentation and Records

Maintain the following for audit and tracking: Asset Request Email, Asset Allocation Form,
Asset Return Records, and Asset Disposal Records.

## 11. Compliance

Failure to comply may result in revocation of IT access, disciplinary action, or financial
liability in case of negligence.

## 12. Review and Updates

This SOP will be reviewed periodically by the IT department to ensure alignment with
organizational policies and audit requirements.

An IT Usage Policy for Accel Digital Media Private Limited is a formal set of rules
explaining how employees should use company technology such as computers, internet, email
systems, software, and company data. It helps protect the organization from security risks,
data breaches, and misuse of IT resources.

## 1. Purpose

- Ensure secure and responsible use of IT resources
- Protect company data and confidential information
- Prevent misuse of company systems
- Maintain network and system security

## 2. Scope

Applies to all employees, managers, contractors and consultants, and temporary staff and
interns. It covers all company IT resources: computers and laptops, company networks and
Wi-Fi, email systems, software applications, and cloud services.

## 3. Acceptable Use

Employees are allowed to:

- Use company IT resources for official business purposes
- Access authorized applications and websites
- Send and receive professional emails
- Store work-related documents in approved systems

Limited personal use may be allowed if it does not interfere with work or compromise
security.

## 4. Prohibited Use

Employees must not:

- Install unauthorized software
- Access illegal, harmful, or inappropriate websites
- Download pirated software or copyrighted materials
- Use company systems for personal business activities
- Attempt to hack, bypass security, or access restricted data
- Share confidential company information without permission
- Use wired/wireless internet intended for company IT assets on personal devices

## 5. Password and Account Security

Employees must use strong passwords, keep them confidential, change them regularly, lock
devices when leaving their workstation, and avoid sharing login credentials.

## 6. Email and Internet Usage

- Use company email for official communication
- Avoid sending spam, offensive, or misleading emails
- Be cautious when opening unknown attachments or links
- Use the internet responsibly and for work-related purposes
- Wireless connections to mobile phones are permitted only for official purposes and require
  special approval

## 7. Data Protection and Confidentiality

Employees must protect sensitive company data, store information only on authorized systems,
avoid copying company data to personal devices, and follow company data privacy and security
policies.

## 8. Use of Personal Devices (BYOD)

Use of personal devices for company work is **not permitted**.

## 9. Monitoring and Compliance

The company reserves the right to monitor internet usage, monitor emails and system
activity, and audit IT systems to ensure policy compliance.

## 10. Policy Violations

Violations may result in a warning or disciplinary action, suspension of system access,
termination of employment, or legal action if necessary.

## 11. Policy Review

The policy should be reviewed regularly and updated to address new technology, cyber
threats, and regulatory requirements.

## 1. Purpose

Define the standard process for procurement, allocation, usage, tracking, maintenance, and
disposal of IT assets within Accel Digital, ensuring accountability, security, and cost
optimization.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all Accel Digital employees, contractors, and consultants, and all IT assets
including:

- Laptops (Windows & MacBook)
- Desktops & monitors (e.g., BenQ screens)
- Mobile devices
- Accessories (headsets, docking stations, etc.)
- Software licenses (Microsoft 365, OS, tools)
- Network and meeting room devices

## 3. Objectives

- Maintain 100% asset visibility and tracking
- Ensure secure and compliant usage
- Optimize asset lifecycle and cost
- Enable smooth onboarding and offboarding

## 4. Asset Lifecycle Process

### 4.1 Procurement
- All IT assets must be procured through IT & Procurement.
- Vendor comparison (minimum 2 quotes) is mandatory.
- Approval required from reporting manager + budget owner.
- PO must be signed and stamped before release.

### 4.2 Asset Allocation
- Assets are allocated based on role (e.g., MacBook for design/dev roles).
- All assets must be asset-tagged and entered into the asset register / SharePoint tracker.
- User must acknowledge via the Asset Handover Form.

### 4.3 Asset Usage Policy
- Assets are strictly for official use.
- Users must not install unauthorized software, not share company devices, and ensure device
  security (password, lock, antivirus).

### 4.4 Asset Tracking & Audit
- IT team maintains a centralized asset tracker (SharePoint/Excel).
- Mandatory audits: quarterly physical verification and random checks by IT/Admin.
- Any mismatch must be escalated immediately.

### 4.5 Maintenance & Support
- Issues must be logged via IT helpdesk/email.
- Only authorized vendors/IT team can repair assets.
- Buffer stock (spare laptops) to be maintained for emergencies.

### 4.6 Asset Transfer
- Inter-user transfers must be approved by IT, updated in the asset register, and
  acknowledged by the new user.

### 4.7 Asset Return (Exit / Replacement)
- Mandatory before Full & Final settlement.
- IT must ensure physical verification, data backup (if needed), and complete data wiping.

### 4.8 Data Security
- All devices must comply with company security policies and antivirus/OS updates.
- No official data on personal devices.
- USB and external storage usage should be controlled.

### 4.9 Asset Disposal
- Assets to be disposed only after approval.
- Data must be permanently erased.
- Follow e-waste compliance standards.
